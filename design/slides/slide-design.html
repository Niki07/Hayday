Design + UX

Designed for a phone, in a field, by someone with ten minutes and a reason to be nervous.

Mobile-first, one-handed

  • 52 px tap targets, one primary action per screen
  • Four-step flow: Photo → Review → Result → Letter
  • Camera and gallery as separate buttons; verified at 375 px, no horizontal scroll

Bilingual by design

  • Typed i18n dictionary, every string in English and Spanish (formal usted)
  • One-tap toggle on every page, choice persisted
  • Letter, PDF and CSV follow the chosen language

Trust made visible

  • Each finding: plain title, severity, the arithmetic, a linked Labor Code citation
  • "Checked against California law" strip on every result; rules page; estimates labeled
  • Rate source and verification date shown on screen

No dead ends

  • No stub? Enter what you know (and learn it is itself a violation)
  • Photo failed? Retry or enter by hand; low-confidence reads flagged for review
  • Progress states for 7–15 s AI calls; county-routed legal aid on the result

Visual system

  • One accent drawn from the logo; cream and brown brand; Inter
  • Calm and professional, not a kids' app: money and dignity
  • Print stylesheet produces a clean two-page PDF

Accessibility + privacy

  • Labeled inputs, aria-live status, visible focus, screen-reader notes on external links
  • Worker initials only in organizer view; nothing stored, said on every page
  • Terms page in plain words: an estimate, not legal advice
Tested every button clicked end-to-end on the live site · sample flow with no network · camera and upload tested on a real phone · Spanish on every screen
