## Prompt
Solve a real challenge involving agriculture, transportation, education, or community life in the Central Valley.
Any app, game or website. Show us who it helps.

## Judging criteria
| Criterion | Weight | What they ask |
|---|---|---|
| Innovation & Creativity | 30% | Uniqueness and originality. How different is it from existing solutions? |
| Technical Implementation | 25% | Complexity and efficiency. How well executed technically? |
| Relevance & Impact | 20% | How well it addresses the problem and its potential real-world impact. |
| Presentation | 15% | Clear and compelling demo. |
| Design & UX | 10% | Well-designed and user-friendly. |

## Implications for planning
- Innovation is the heaviest weight: the idea must be visibly different from "an existing app but for the Valley".
- Technical is second: real data or real APIs beat mock screens. One genuinely clever mechanism is enough.
- "Show us who it helps" plus Impact 20% plus Presentation 15%: the demo needs a named user and a story.
- 8 hours and 2 people: idea locked within the first 30 minutes, demo script running end to end by hour 4.
